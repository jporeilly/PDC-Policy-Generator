1. LICENCE AND PERMITTED USE

This software reads the Classification Registry exported by the PDC
Glossary Generator and manages the Data Identification side of that
contract: it authors import-ready Data Patterns and Dictionaries,
reconciles term bindings against a Pentaho Data Catalog instance,
deploys the methods over the catalog's import API, and checks deployed
methods for drift. It is used in Pentaho training workshops and
pre-sales engagements, and is provided for use on authorised machines
and by authorised personnel only. You may install and use it for that
purpose. You may not sell, sublicense, rent or otherwise commercially
exploit the software, and you may not remove or alter any proprietary
notice it contains.


2. YOUR REGISTRY AND YOUR CATALOG

The software reads Classification Registry files. A Registry describes
your data estate: table and column names, business terms, governed
tags, sensitivity levels, and detection seeds - value patterns induced
from profiled data and reference-value lists that may contain actual
values sampled from your estate. Treat Registry files with the same
care as the data they describe.

Unlike a purely reporting tool, this software can WRITE to the catalog
you configure: Deploy imports identification methods, re-stamps term
bindings, and can trigger a data-identification job scoped to entities
you choose; Retire removes methods. Nothing is deployed, changed or
removed until you explicitly choose it in the app, and everything the
software authors is presented for review first.

You are responsible for having the authority to load each Registry,
to connect to the catalog with the account you configure, and for the
effect of the methods you choose to deploy. Use an account scoped to
what the lifecycle actually needs.

4. NO AI ASSISTANCE - DETERMINISTIC BY DESIGN

This software contains no artificial intelligence system and calls no
language model, local or hosted. Every regex and reference list it
emits was induced deterministically from profiled data by the Glossary
Generator's scan and travels inside the Registry; this software
arranges those seeds into the catalog's own import formats without
generating content of its own. The same input always produces the
same output.

This is deliberate: identification methods decide what a catalog
treats as personal or sensitive data, and a probabilistic system
authoring them would place the resulting deployment in a materially
different regulatory position. Do not modify the software to add
AI-generated methods without recording that change and its
consequences yourself.


5. DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY

  (a) Data kept on your machine. The software keeps no state of its
      own beside the code: it reads the Registry files you point it
      at, and its one file output - a seed request for terms that
      still need detection seeds - is written beside the loaded
      Registry, where the Glossary Generator looks for it.

  (b) Credentials are never persisted. The catalog credentials you
      enter for reconcile/deploy are held in memory for the session
      and are gone when the application closes. Nothing writes them
      to disk.

  (c) Your catalog. Deploy sends the authored identification methods
      - names, regexes, reference values, tag stampings and term
      bindings - to the Pentaho Data Catalog instance you configure,
      and can trigger identification jobs there. This is the purpose
      of the tool; nothing is sent until you choose to deploy.

  (d) Personal data in the Registry. Detection seeds may include
      value patterns and reference lists derived from profiled estate
      data, which can themselves reveal or contain personal data.
      Load and store Registry files only where you are entitled to
      process that data, and delete exported artifacts (method zips,
      seed requests) when no longer needed.

  (e) Controller. The organisation operating the software determines
      the purposes and means of this processing and is the data
      controller. It is responsible for its own privacy notice,
      lawful basis, retention periods and transfer mechanisms. The
      authors of the software supply a tool and operate no service on
      your behalf.

  (f) Your rights. Subject to applicable law you have the right to
      request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your
      personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to
      data portability, and to complain to your data protection
      supervisory authority. Exercise these rights with the
      organisation named in its privacy notice. Locally held Registry
      copies and exports can be removed at any time by deleting the
      files.

  (g) Data minimisation. Load only the Registry you need, deploy only
      the methods you intend to govern with, and scope identification
      jobs to the entities that need them.
